ABOUT CASA DECOR

A showroom approach to complete home decoration.

Casa Decor brings furniture and interior finishing solutions together—so customers can plan, customize and complete their home with clarity.

What we do

Casa Decor is positioned as a complete home-decoration & interior solutions showroom—covering furniture and key interior elements such as flooring, wall panels, ceilings, lighting integration, electrical fittings and finishing details.

We focus on cohesive results: proportions, materials, color direction and customization options that fit the space.

Customization

Selected products support customization—fabric options, multiple colors, marble options and dimensional adjustments depending on the piece.
