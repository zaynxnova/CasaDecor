PRIVACY

Privacy & communication

A simple, transparent policy for inquiries.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect the information you provide—such as your name, phone number, email (if provided), and your message—to respond and support your request.

How we use it

We use inquiry information only for communication related to your request (product details, customization, pricing, consultation, or services).

Data retention

Inquiry messages may be retained for follow-up and record-keeping. You can request removal by contacting Casa Decor using the contact details on the website.

Updates

This page may be updated as the business adds new services and communication channels.
